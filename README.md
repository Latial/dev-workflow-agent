- **accepted** — you merged the PR as-is. **edited** — you merged after your own follow-up commits. **rejected** — closed without merging.
- **Acceptance rate** = (accepted + edited) / judged PRs. Track "edited" separately in your notes; a high edit rate is a prompt-quality signal.
- The failure-mode taxonomy above is a *starter*. Renaming and splitting categories as patterns emerge in your transcripts is exactly the analytical skill Chapter 1 is after. Note `test-gamed` — it's the sneakiest one; Phase 9 tells you how to catch it.
